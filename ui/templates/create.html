💡 How to Write Effective Content Prompts

✅ Excellent Prompts

Specific + Value:
"The 5 AI tools that increased our startup's productivity by 200% in 2024"
Personal Story + Lesson:
"How I raised $2M for my SaaS startup without a single pitch deck"
Controversial + Data:
"Why 90% of startup accelerators are wasting founders' time (and what to do instead)"
Problem + Solution:
"Our customer acquisition cost was $500. Here's how we reduced it to $50"

❌ Weak Prompts

Too vague:
"AI tips for startups"
→ No specific value or outcome
Generic advice:
"How to be successful"
→ Too broad, no actionable insights
No context:
"Our product launch"
→ No hook or specific angle
Self-promotional:
"Why our startup is amazing"
→ No value for audience

🚀 Pro Tips for Maximum Engagement

📊 Include Numbers & Data:
  • • "3 mistakes that cost us $100K"
  • • "From 0 to 10K users in 6 months"
  • • "5 metrics every founder should track"
🎯 Target Specific Pain Points:
  • • "Struggling with user retention?"
  • • "Can't find product-market fit?"
  • • "Tired of rejected investor pitches?"
📖 Share Personal Stories:
  • • "The day we almost shut down"
  • • "My biggest hiring mistake"
  • • "When our server crashed on launch day"
💡 Offer Actionable Insights:
  • • "Step-by-step guide to..."
  • • "The exact framework we used"
  • • "Copy this strategy for your startup"

🎨 Content Formula for Maximum Impact

[Specific Outcome/Number] + [Personal Experience/Story] + [Actionable Value]
Example: "How I grew my SaaS from $0 to $50K MRR in 8 months (and the 3 strategies that made the difference)"
Breakdown: Specific outcome ($50K MRR in 8 months) + Personal experience (I grew my SaaS) + Actionable value (3 strategies)
